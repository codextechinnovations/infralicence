# Strategy and Observations

## Key Observations
- **Qualification Relaxation**: Civil Engineering degree/diploma holders may get relaxation in some work completion requirements. Individual applicants with BE Civil/Diploma may upload qualification certificates instead of certain experience proofs.
- **Bottlenecks**: The most challenging parts of the application are typically obtaining work completion certificates, solvency certificates, and linking technical qualifications.

## Strategic Suggestions
- **Starting Point**: For a new LLP without a large project history, 4th Class or 3rd Class registration is the easiest starting point.
- **Growth**: Upgrade the registration class over time using completed government or private works.
