# Practical Checklist for LLP Registration

## Company Documents
- [ ] LLP Incorporation Certificate
- [ ] LLP Agreement
- [ ] PAN
- [ ] GST
- [ ] Address proof

## Partner Documents
- [ ] Aadhaar
- [ ] PAN
- [ ] Photos
- [ ] DSC (Digital Signature)

## Financial
- [ ] Bank Solvency Certificate
- [ ] CA Networth/Turnover certificate

## Technical
- [ ] Civil engineer qualification certificate
- [ ] Experience/work completion certificates

## Payments
- [ ] Registration fee challan
- [ ] QR license fee
- [ ] Welfare fund fee
