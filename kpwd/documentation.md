# Documentation Requirements

## Core Documents (Common Across All Classes)
- GST Certificate
- PAN Card
- Aadhaar/Address proof
- Income Tax details
- Chartered Accountant certificate
- Solvency Certificate
- Work completion certificates
- Engineering degree/diploma certificate
- ₹100 stamp paper affidavit
- Registration fee challan
- QR-based contractor license fee

## LLP / Partnership / Company Requirements

### Partnership Firms
- Partnership Deed
- Firm registration certificate
- Firm PAN
- Partners PAN & Aadhaar
- GST certificate

### Companies
- MOA & AOA
- Certificate of Incorporation
- Director KYC documents
- Company PAN
- GST certificate
